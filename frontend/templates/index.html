📋 Workflow Guide

Recommended Order of Operations

▼

To maintain a clean, properly tagged, and synchronized music library with zero metadata conflicts, execute tasks in this sequence:

1
Sync Spotify CSVs

Run Exportify to download the latest Spotify playlist CSV exports into exports/.

2
Audit & Compare

Click Sync in Settings to detect missing tracks, extra files, duration discrepancies, and tag mismatches.

3
Download & Clean Extras

Download missing tracks using SpotiFLAC (⬇ Download All), and delete obsolete local files (🗑 Delete).

4
Fix Tags & Filenames

Use the one-click Fix buttons in the discrepancy tables to align Artist, Album, Title, and Filename tags with Spotify.

5
Strip Junk Metadata

Strip track/disc numbers, stray dates, comments, and lyrics to ensure audio files have a clean container before re-tagging.

6
Import Year & Genre

Write verified release years and genres from Spotify/Exportify CSVs directly into local audio tags.

7
Standardize FLAC Covers

Scan albums to find the highest-resolution cover art, unify tracks, and ensure Front Cover (Type 3) tags.

8
Scan ReplayGain

Run rsgain 2.0 loudness normalization across your library as the final finishing step.
